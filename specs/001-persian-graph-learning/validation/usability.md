# Phase 8 — five-student usability / T057

**Pending; zero participants tested.** No student observations or success rates were fabricated.
A developer's browser walkthrough is not this study. Full release still requires it.

## Prepared protocol

Recruit five grade-12 students, record anonymous participant IDs P1–P5 and device/browser,
with consent handled by the teacher. Start at the default site state; supply the task only:
“Find a practice question, answer it, then find its saved result.” Do not point at controls
or explain the navigation. Observe independently; record completion, time, help requests,
wrong navigation, readability/touch/focus problems and the participant's short comments.
Then ask each to adjust motion and locate the way to delete that chosen result. Do not score
learning ability or personality. Keep real student responses out of the repository.

Primary success: at least **4/5** locate/answer a practice item and find its report unaided.
Report failures and fix observed blockers before repeating any justified check. No automatic
approval, synthetic participant or estimated 4/5 outcome is acceptable.
